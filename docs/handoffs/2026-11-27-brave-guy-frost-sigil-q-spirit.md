Local H5 candidate on APHASEN. Not pushed or published. Owner supplied a photo of a frosted blue-violet snowflake pendant as the reference, and clarified he wants an actual little spirit version of Nightmare as the floating cannon.

The supplied reference is not shipped. Reduced vortex helix opacity only; the barrier/other placement behavior, lifetime, damage and skill mechanics stay the same. Existing two cached textures and layer count are unchanged.
- `02_brave_guy/js/nightmare-drone-art.js`: dedicated Q spirit silhouette with oversized head, floating short body, original fighter palette and small wings. The child aircraft no longer clone or skin the humanoid model. Long narrow crossed wave silhouettes travel directly in their launch direction. The firing interval, count, hit radius, multiplier and existing target selection remain unchanged from the previous candidate. Projectiles do not home after launch.
- `js/angel-ice-fx.js`, `js/guest-combat.js`, `js/main.js`, `play.html` use chained cache keys `20261127frostsigil` and `20261127qspirit`. The original combo presentation remains unchanged.
