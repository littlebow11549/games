# 2026-11-27 — Little Angel shoulder companion

The normal Brave Guy route now uses a dedicated, textured, skinned Little Angel model for the floating shoulder companion, rather than the old primitive placeholder. It derives from the current playable Little Angel model, whose pink hair, hat bells, star marking, wing curls, white/red jacket, and blue skirt are the visual reference. The model is genuine volumetric geometry and plays the original bone-based idle. The shoulder anchor/rescue approach are preserved, with a small additive hover/bank and a restrained three-quarter turn so the face stays readable from the game camera. The two alternative guest companions, student and Brave Guy spirits, still use their existing appearances and mechanics.

- Source: `02_brave_guy/assets/runtime/stage-art/brave_guy_charactor02_angel_motion.glb` (SHA256 `a36f41de6852df5c7dc014866ee013a94d4273ee620db47159cfa9830f4a1568`), backed by the original Little Angel concept turnarounds in `assets/source/pic/concept/`. The source and playable model were not modified.
- Runtime: `02_brave_guy/assets/runtime/stage-art/brave_guy_charactor02_angel_companion.glb` (SHA256 `4582e2b12c6d004e69c4d20ed5cf13ca620990f94110b2dd41278b1481949a52`). One skinned mesh and base texture at 768 px, stock idle only, 7,622 triangles compared to the original 28,098, 766,740 bytes compared to 4,948,940. The unlit painted palette retains the artwork's bright cel colors despite stage tint; the geometry still turns and casts shadow. UV-boundary-constrained reduction preserves seam islands and the color texture.
- Integration: `manifest.models.angel` loads the lightweight model once at boot. `updateAngel()` adds hover only to the new GLTF visual; the existing procedural guide remains the fetch-failure fallback. No changes to dialogue or rescue/character abilities. Reproduce with `python tools/make_game02_angel_companion.py`.
- QA: `node tools/tests/game02-angel-companion.cjs` covers geometry dimensions, single-skin draw budget, asset size, idle animation and animated shoulder/rescue positioning with unchanged guest/fallback mode behavior. Renderer-independent reference/companion idle views compared from 0°, 30° and 75°; the production integration additionally showed the textured character in the browser.
