# Brave Guy campaign art direction and combat presentation

Status: local H5 candidate on APHASEN, not pushed or published. Art review by the owner is pending. This follows the owner's Pokkén Tournament DX art-quality benchmark, not its characters, logo, or fighting-game mechanics. No third-party reference artwork is included.

## Implemented
- Per-level compositing for all 16 level IDs, including the separate campus pipeline. Authored warm/key direction, shadow tone, fog depth and key/fill balance let the campus read as daylight, the forest as moist green depth, the office as cooler architecture with warm fixture pools, and the finale as violet depth. Existing maze cells, art geometry, baked floor and collision data stay intact. The old whole-canvas filters have been replaced by world lighting/fog and palette-matched glaze outside the HUD. Story wisps, fireflies, embers and small reflections use existing particle counts and reused textures.
- Refined directional brush edges on the sweep and linear casts, plus separate wind pressure, contact and fragment stages on heavy hit art. Preserve the owner's original light-impact atlas and anchor the heavy build to it. Heavy/counter/guard orientations use the projected source/target direction, not the hit sprite's offset toward the camera. No combat damage, range or timings changed.
- Original clipped lettering plates for stage numbers, color accents for cast labels, and coordinated boss/player bars. The owner explicitly kept the previous combo presentation; its original atlas, typography, placement and animation are unchanged. Existing approved skill artwork remains the primary title for all three characters. Action icons and joystick layout are unchanged. Full-health non-target mobs stop drawing their bars in dense waves, but injured/locked foes and bosses retain their bars.
- Soft contacts have a stronger foot anchor and a wider falloff, still one instanced draw. The visual pass adds no real-time lights or effect instances.
