F replaces the generic ring stack with three layered helical currents, inward-swept snow and crystals and a distinct expanding finale. R leaves the pink/cyan repelling barrier and five positions in place, replacing generic gold domes with compact snow-crystal pops and retaining a gold boundary ring. Q and non-Angel skills are outside this handoff.

Open `02_brave_guy/play.html` and select Little Angel in the character picker. No standalone preview or raster art.
- **Art source:** Procedurally modeled mesh ribbons, low strips, faceted crystals and branching snowflakes in a blue/white palette with pink highlights, using the existing Three runtime. No new external images, textures, or dependencies. The crystal and snow batches use instanced geometry. F's persistent effect uses 13 or fewer mesh draw batches and 74 combined instances, no shadow/light cost. All use world depth, disable depth writes, and dispose owned resources on expiry or game cleanup.

F still draws the same targets inward for four seconds, ticks every .22s and applies the existing 6× final damage; the shorter .72s finale continues as visual-only. Animation time follows simulation time; phase clears remove all ice effects.
